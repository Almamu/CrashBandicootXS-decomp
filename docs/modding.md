# Modding

The game's content is data: the levels, their rooms and themes, the
level-select pages, the actor categories (the vehicle and boss stages),
the bosses' rewards, the music, the cutscenes, the credits, the text in
its six languages and the graphics. A plain `make` builds whatever data
the tree has, and `make compare` checks that the original data still
gives the original ROM byte for byte. A mod is other data: it needs no
code change and no `baserom.gba`.

## The sample mod

[`mods/sample/`](../mods/sample/) is a small mod that uses most of what is
moddable, and the worked example for this page. It adds a fifth world
(a fifth level-select page) after Neo Cortex's:

| what | where it comes from | the doc |
|---|---|---|
| a new song, `sample_tune`, played by a new level theme and by the new cutscene | an XM the mod writes (16-row patterns; a new instrument with its own synthesized sample, and a pool instrument) | [audio.md, "Adding a song"](./audio.md#adding-a-song) |
| a new theme, `THEME_SAMPLE` | jungle's picture and colour cycle, the new song | [levels.md, "Adding a theme"](./levels.md#adding-a-theme) |
| a new room, `sample_grove/main` (room 41) | jungle jam's room under its own symbols, one 8x8 block repainted (a new tile) | [levels.md, "Adding a room"](./levels.md#adding-a-room) |
| a recoloured actor category, `CATEGORY_SAMPLE_ICE` | snow crash's polar stage with its own OBJ palette and BG palette cycle | [levels.md, "Category graphics"](./levels.md#category-graphics) |
| three numbered levels, "sample grove", "sample slope", "sample sewer" | the new room, the new category's stage, just in slime's room | [levels.md, "Adding a world"](./levels.md#adding-a-world) |
| a boss level, "sample showdown" | Dingodile's room, `"boss_kind": "DINGODILE"` (the boss HUD) and an `"on_clear"` that plays the new cutscene and marks it cleared | [levels.md, "Boss levels"](./levels.md#boss-levels) |
| a partial level-select page and its number icons 21-23 | world 4 of `"worlds"`, opened when world 3's levels are cleared; new frames of sprite bank 51 | [levels.md, "Partial pages"](./levels.md#partial-pages), [graphics.md, "New frames"](./graphics.md#new-frames) |
| the 64 Kbit save | 29 levels, past the 4 Kbit save's 28 | [levels.md, "The save"](./levels.md#the-save) |
| a new cutscene, `CUTSCENE_SAMPLE_CLEARED` | a picture the mod draws, a page of text | [data.md, "A new cutscene"](./data.md#a-new-cutscene) |
| the levels' names and the cutscene's text in the six languages | entries of `games/xs/text/crashxs.pot` and the five `.po` files | [text.md, "A new level's name"](./text.md#a-new-levels-name) |

Every asset is generated by the mod or derived from the tree's own data
(the palettes are the ROM's recoloured), so it carries no copied art and
no copyrighted music.

### Trying it

    make mod MOD=mods/sample

applies the mod to a copy of the tree in `build/mods/sample`
(`tools/apply_mod.py`, whose code is [`crashdata.mods`](../packages/crashdata/src/crashdata/mods/__init__.py))
and builds it there with a
plain `make`. The modded ROM is `build/mods/sample/crashbandicootxs.gba`
(16 MB: the new data goes past the original's 8 MB). Your own tree is
not changed. In the modded tree,

    make text-check
    python3 tools/gfx_manifest.py check
    python3 mods/sample/boottest.py

check the translations and the graphics manifest, and play the ROM in
headless mGBA (`mgba`, its SDL frontend, on `PATH`: 0.10.5, which CI
builds from source; see `tools/boottest.py`,
[`buildtools.boottest`](../packages/buildtools/src/buildtools/boottest.py)): the new room and its song,
the category's palettes on the screen, the fifth page and its boss entry,
the boss HUD, the cutscene after the boss and the 8 KiB save. CI
(`.github/workflows/mods.yml`) does all of this on every PR, so a change
that breaks modding fails there even when `make compare` passes.

In the game, the fifth page opens once the levels of the four ROM worlds
are cleared; the boot test pokes that progress into the save instead of
playing it.

### Writing a mod

A mod is a folder with a `mod.py` and, optionally, a `files/` folder:

- `files/` is copied over the tree, at the same paths: new files, or
  replacements of the tree's. XS's data is under `games/xs/`
  (`files/games/xs/graphics/...`, `files/games/xs/data/levels/...`) and
  the code under `engine/` (`files/engine/src/...`), as in the tree
  (#1167, [file_layout.md](file_layout.md));
- the paths `mod.py` gives the helpers (`tree.path(...)`,
  `tree.json(...)`) are the tree's too: `games/xs/sound/gax_manifest.json`,
  `engine/src/game/...`;
- `mod.py` defines `apply(tree)`, which edits the copy's data with the
  helpers of `crashdata.mods.Tree` (`tools/apply_mod.py`'s): `tree.levels()` and
  `tree.json(path)` edit a JSON file, `tree.add_graphics(...)` adds
  `games/xs/graphics/manifest.json` entries, `tree.copy_room(...)` copies a room
  under new symbols, `tree.add_level_name(...)` and
  `tree.add_cutscene_text(...)` add text in the six languages,
  `tree.run(...)` runs a tool. `mod.py` runs with the copy's `tools/` on
  `sys.path`, so it can import a tool by its old name (`import
  jasc_pal`, which is `crashdata.common.jasc_pal`, as the sample does) or
  the packages themselves (`from crashdata.common import jasc_pal`;
  [file_layout.md](file_layout.md), "The Python packages").

`tools/apply_mod.py MOD DEST` applies it to a new copy at `DEST`, and
`make mod MOD=<folder>` does that and builds it. `make data-check
MOD=<folder>` applies it and checks the copy's data without building:
every file against its JSON Schema, then the checks the build makes
([editors.md](./editors.md), "Checking the data"). To work on the data
directly instead, edit the tree itself, following the docs above, and
run `make`; `make compare` then fails, as it should. The editor
(`make editor`, [editors.md](./editors.md)) edits a mod's tree
(`build/mods/<name>`) the same way: new, resized and deleted rooms, a
room's kind and blend; applying the mod again makes the tree
anew, so what is kept goes into `mod.py`.

## What a mod can change

| area | moddable | the doc |
|---|---|---|
| levels | rooms (pictures, collision, entities), the level table, themes and their colour cycles, level-select pages and what opens them, names | [levels.md](./levels.md) |
| vehicle and boss stages | actor categories: spawn lists, numbers, cell animation, sky, OBJ palette, palette cycle, sprite sheet, animation table | [levels.md, "Actor categories"](./levels.md#actor-categories) |
| bosses | which boss a level has (`"boss_kind"`) and what clearing a level does (`"on_clear"`) | [levels.md, "Boss levels"](./levels.md#boss-levels) |
| the save | grows with the levels (64 Kbit past 28), versioned by a hash of the level data | [levels.md, "The save"](./levels.md#the-save) |
| text | every string, in six languages; the credits | [text.md](./text.md) |
| cutscenes | slides, pictures, timing, music, text | [data.md, "Cutscenes"](./data.md#cutscenes) |
| music | new songs from XM files, on the ROM's instruments or new ones (an XM's own samples, or WAVs); new sound effects with their own samples; which song plays where; the music editor plays them and edits the tables, the songs' cells and the samples | [audio.md](./audio.md), [editors.md, "The music editor"](./editors.md#the-music-editor) |
| graphics | the graphics of the manifest, new ones, sprite banks and frames, palettes | [graphics.md](./graphics.md) |

## What a mod can't do yet

These are code, or not built yet:

- **Editors.** The level, cutscene and music editors (`make editor`,
  [editors.md](./editors.md)) edit a mod's tree (`build/mods/<name>`, as
  `make mod` makes it) as they edit the game's: the sample mod's
  `sample_tune` plays in the music editor, its own instrument too. What
  they don't do yet is in each editor's "Not yet"; for the music, an XM
  is still written in a tracker (the editor edits its cells, and plays
  them as GAX2 will), and a ROM instrument's envelope or loop is the
  manifest's (the "Data files" tab).
- **New behaviour.** A new actor kind, boss kind, power, category type or
  entity type is code: the data reaches what the existing kinds draw and
  where they spawn, not what they do (levels.md, "Category sprite
  sheets" and "Boss levels").
- **New sprite frames outside bank 51's animation 0.** Other banks need
  their C to take new frames (graphics.md, "New frames").
- **More than 60 levels**, the 64 Kbit save's largest record.
- **N-Tranced.** The modding pipeline is Crash Bandicoot XS's; N-Tranced's
  data isn't decompiled yet ([n_tranced.md](./n_tranced.md)).
